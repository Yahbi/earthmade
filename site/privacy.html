Privacy & Terms

Last updated 2 August 2026

Who we are

Atelier Earthmade is an atelier commissioning monolithic natural-stone bathing vessels. We sell direct to private clients and to trade. All enquiries are handled by the atelier through the forms on this site.

What we collect

  • When you enquire: your name, email, city or country, the piece you are interested in, and anything you choose to write to us.
  • When you reserve: additionally your telephone number and delivery address, because a stone vessel cannot be quoted or shipped without them.
  • We do not collect payment card details on this site. Payment is taken by a secure hosted checkout or a payment link.

Why we hold it, and for how long

To answer your enquiry, prepare a written quotation including freight and any duty estimate, produce and deliver your commission, and meet our tax and customs record-keeping obligations. Enquiry records are kept for two years; order records for as long as accounting and customs law requires.

Who else sees it

Only those who must, to complete your order: our form provider, our payment processor, the workshop carving your piece, our freight forwarder and customs broker, and our insurer in the event of a claim. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Third parties on this page

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is transmitted to Google when the page loads. We use no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking on this site.

Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct or erase it, or object to our use of it. Email atelier@atelierearthmade.com or write to us through the enquiry form, and we will respond within one month. If you are in the EU or the UK you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

Terms of sale — the essentials

  • Made to order. Every vessel is cut from a natural block after you reserve. Nothing is held in stock.
  • Payment. In full at secure checkout, handled by Shopify. Nothing is ordered, and no block is committed, until your payment has cleared to us.
  • The three-day hold. When you purchase, your order is held for three days while we assess the logistics for your address — access, floor level, whether rigging is required, and the delivery route — and confirm that plan to you in writing. No block is committed to the workshop during the hold. Only once the hold ends and your payment has cleared is a block selected and cut for you.
  • Cancellation — it runs both ways. Within 72 hours of your payment clearing, either of us may cancel. You may cancel for any reason at all. We may cancel if the workshop or the logistics for your address cannot be confirmed. In either case you are refunded in full, and this is the same window as the three-day hold above — one window, not two. After it closes your block is bought and committed, cutting begins, and the commission cannot be cancelled or returned unless we agree otherwise with you in writing by email. This is why the sale is final: the piece is cut from a block selected for you and cannot be resold as stock. To cancel, email atelier@atelierearthmade.com — the time your email is sent is the time that counts.
  • Your statutory rights are unaffected if a piece arrives late, damaged, faulty or not as described.
  • Price includes freight, crating, export documentation, transit insurance with the sum insured stated in writing on your order, and all import duty and customs charges. There is no separate shipping charge and no border bill.
  • Duties and customs are ours. Atelier Earthmade is the importer of record. We clear each vessel in our own name and pay all import duty and customs charges before delivery. Nothing further is payable on delivery. Where we are registered to collect it, state and local sales tax is calculated and displayed at checkout before you pay; it is the only amount ever added to the listed price. Checkout delivers to addresses in the United States, and the all-in price above applies to US delivery only. International commissions are accepted by enquiry rather than through checkout: freight, import duty and local taxes differ by country, so they are quoted individually and confirmed in writing before any payment is taken. The duty and tax position for an international order is set out in that written quotation and may differ from the US terms above.
  • Delivery is by appointment to the ground-floor threshold. Rigging, craning, stair carries and placement within the building are excluded and quoted separately.
  • On delivery photograph the crate before it is opened and inspect the piece before you sign. If it has been damaged you must refuse the delivery — do not accept it and do not sign for it. Refusal is the event that permits the transit insurance claim and the case against the carrier; a damaged piece that is accepted and signed for closes that route. Note the damage on the carrier's paperwork and tell us the same day. If the piece is sound, keep all packaging until it is confirmed so.
  • Floor loading is the responsibility of your own architect or structural engineer. We provide exact weight, footprint and load-spread figures at no charge.
  • Natural material. Colour, veining and translucency will differ from every image shown. All imagery on this site is computer-generated visualisation, illustrative of material and form only. Natural fissures, vein lines and inclusions are characteristics of stone, not faults.
  • Cover. Each vessel is insured by Atelier Earthmade for the whole journey, the sum insured is stated in writing on your order, and that cover runs to the moment of delivery — you carry no transit risk and pay no part of the premium. Atelier Earthmade does not offer its own structural guarantee on the stone after delivery. Where the workshop that made your piece provides a manufacturer's warranty, we pass it through to you in writing with the piece, on that workshop's terms — these vary by workshop and some offer none at all, so we will tell you exactly what applies to your vessel before you buy. Nothing here covers staining, chipping from impact, thermal shock, incorrect cleaning, failure to re-seal, or movement or rigging by others. Your statutory rights if a piece arrives damaged, faulty or not as described are unaffected.
  • Lead times are good-faith estimates, not guarantees. If we will miss the window you will be offered a definite revised date or a full refund, your choice, in writing and before the window ends.
