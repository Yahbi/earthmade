Privacy & Terms

Last updated 31 July 2026

Who we are

Earth Made is an atelier commissioning monolithic natural-stone bathing vessels. We sell direct to private clients and to trade. All enquiries are handled by the atelier through the forms on this site.

What we collect

  • When you enquire: your name, email, city or country, the piece you are interested in, and anything you choose to write to us.
  • When you reserve: additionally your telephone number and delivery address, because a stone vessel cannot be quoted or shipped without them.
  • We do not collect payment card details on this site. Payment is taken by a secure hosted checkout or a payment link.

Why we hold it, and for how long

To answer your enquiry, prepare a written quotation including freight and any duty estimate, produce and deliver your commission, and meet our tax and customs record-keeping obligations. Enquiry records are kept for two years; order records for as long as accounting and customs law requires.

Who else sees it

Only those who must, to complete your order: our form provider, our payment processor, the workshop carving your piece, our freight forwarder and customs broker, and our insurer in the event of a claim. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Third parties on this page

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is transmitted to Google when the page loads. We use no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking on this site.

Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct or erase it, or object to our use of it. Write to us through the enquiry form and we will respond within one month. If you are in the EU or the UK you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

Terms of sale — the essentials

  • Made to order. Every vessel is cut from a natural block after you reserve. Nothing is held in stock.
  • Payment. 30% to begin carving, the balance before dispatch. A reservation is not a charge.
  • Cancellation. You may cancel for any reason and be refunded in full within 72 hours of your deposit clearing. After that your block is bought and committed, cutting begins, and the commission cannot be cancelled and the deposit is not returnable. The deposit represents identified costs incurred at that moment; if the committed cost proves materially lower, we refund the difference.
  • Your statutory rights are unaffected if a piece arrives late, damaged, faulty or not as described.
  • Price includes worldwide freight, crating, export documentation and all-risk transit insurance to full replacement value. There is no separate shipping charge.
  • Duties. Delivered to a United States address there is nothing further to pay — we import the piece in our own name. Outside the United States, import duty and destination VAT are levied by your own customs authority and settled by you; we send a written estimate for your country before any payment link is issued.
  • Delivery is by appointment to the ground-floor threshold. Rigging, craning, stair carries and placement within the building are excluded and quoted separately.
  • On delivery photograph the crate before it is opened, never sign a clean delivery note where damage exists, and notify us in writing within 24 hours. Keep all packaging until we confirm the piece is accepted — a transit claim depends on it.
  • Floor loading is the responsibility of your own architect or structural engineer. We provide exact weight, footprint and load-spread figures at no charge.
  • Natural material. Colour, veining and translucency will differ from every image shown. Photographs and renders are illustrative of material and form only. Natural fissures, vein lines and inclusions are characteristics of stone, not faults.
  • Guarantee. Ten-year structural guarantee. It does not cover staining, chipping from impact, thermal shock, incorrect cleaning, failure to re-seal, or movement or rigging by others.
  • Lead times are good-faith estimates, not guarantees. If we will miss the window you will be offered a definite revised date or a full refund, your choice, in writing and before the window ends.
