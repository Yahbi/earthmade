VCraft

Nothing is added.
Only removed.

A monolithic bath begins as several tonnes of rock and ends as a vessel you can sit inside. Between those two facts there is no casting, no mould, no joint — only subtraction. The work runs four to twenty weeks depending on the hardness of the stone, some of it machine-assisted, most of it by hand. A block can be lost at any stage — a hidden fissure, a vein that runs wrong — and the work begins again.

01

One block, not several

Every vessel is cut from a single block of natural stone — never assembled, bonded or laminated from pieces, and never stone-effect resin. Our workshops state it plainly: one monolithic boulder, no glue, no reconstruction. It is the first thing we verify before a piece enters the collection.

02

No two blocks alike

Colour, veining and translucency differ from block to block, so no two vessels match. The piece that reaches you will differ from every photograph on this page. That is the material, not a fault.

03

Hollowed by machine and hand

The bulk is taken out by machine; the interior is finished by hand. Every piece in the collection is hand-carved — that is what the workshops build, and it is why a vessel takes weeks rather than days.

04

Honed or polished

Finished to the specification for the piece. A honed surface reads matte and soft underfoot; a polished one reads reflective and cooler. Both are cut into the same solid stone.

05

Sealed before dispatch

An impregnating sealer is applied before the piece leaves the workshop. Porous stones — travertine, sandstone, limestone, onyx — want re-sealing every 12 to 18 months once they are in use.

06

Crated and shipped

Crated for export and shipped by sea, insured in transit for the whole journey, with the sum insured stated in writing on your order. We clear it as importer of record and deliver it to your threshold.

The workshops

Atelier Earthmade does not own a quarry or a workshop, and does not claim to. We are a small house that selects: we work with established stone workshops that still cut monolithic vessels from a single block, and we commission each piece from them for a specific client. Our part is the selection — the stone, the form, the standard a piece must meet before we will accept it, and the responsibility for everything that happens between their bench and your door. We name the material on every vessel. We do not name the workshops.

Every vessel is crated to order and insured in transit, with the sum insured stated in writing on your order. If a piece is damaged on its way to you, we handle the claim with the insurer and the carrier, and you deal only with us.

Made to order

Nothing waits in a warehouse. When you reserve, a block is selected for you and cut — 4 to 20 weeks at the bench depending on the stone and the form. Your dates are confirmed in writing once your block is chosen, not before.

Payment

Checkout is secure and handled by Shopify: payment in full. Your order is then held for three days while we assess the logistics for your address — access, floor level, whether rigging is needed, and the delivery route — and confirm that plan to you in writing. Nothing is ordered and no block is committed during that hold. Within 72 hours of payment clearing, either of us may cancel: you for any reason at all, and us if the workshop or the logistics cannot be confirmed. Either way you are refunded in full. It is one window, not two. After it closes your block is bought and cutting begins, and the commission cannot be cancelled or returned unless we agree otherwise with you in writing by email. None of this affects your rights if a piece arrives late, damaged, faulty or not as described.

Freight & insurance

We crate, book and manage the shipment door to door, and insure it in transit. Freight and transit insurance are already inside the price. There is no separate shipping charge to any address in the United States.

Duties & import taxes

One price, delivered. It includes freight, crating, export documentation, transit insurance with the sum insured stated in writing on your order, and all import duty and customs clearance. We are the importer of record: we clear the piece and the duty is ours. Nothing further is ever payable on delivery. Where we are registered to collect it, state sales tax is shown at checkout before you pay. Checkout delivers to addresses in the United States, where this all-in price applies. We do ship internationally — but freight, duty and local tax differ by country, so an international commission begins with a conversation rather than a checkout. Send us an enquiry and we will price it properly for your address.

Delivery

Delivered crated to your ground-floor threshold. Inspect it before you sign. If the piece has been damaged, you must refuse the delivery — do not accept it and do not sign for it. Refusal is what permits the transit insurance claim and the case against the carrier; once a damaged piece is accepted and signed for, that route closes. Note the damage on the carrier's paperwork, photograph what you can, and tell us the same day. If the piece is sound, keep the crate and all packaging until it is confirmed so. Plumbing is connected by your own licensed installer. Setting a piece above the ground floor is rigging work, not delivery — tell us where it must stand and we will quote it with a specialist in your city, or coordinate with your builder.

Care & cover

Sealed before dispatch. Clean with pH-neutral soap and warm water; re-seal every 12 to 18 months. Each vessel is crated to order and insured in transit. That cover runs to the moment of delivery. Atelier Earthmade adds no structural guarantee of its own beyond it; where the workshop that made your piece offers a manufacturer's warranty, we pass it through on their terms and tell you what applies before you buy.

Questions we are asked

Is it really carved from one solid block?

Yes — that is the definition of the collection. Each vessel is bored, hollowed, shaped and honed from one piece of stone: no resin core, no stone veneer over a substrate, no seam and no join in the body. That is a claim about the body of the vessel. Surface sealers are a separate matter — every piece is sealed before despatch, and Aeon’s petrified wood additionally takes an impregnating sealer worked into the face to stabilise its natural fissures.

It is the reason a piece weighs what it does, holds the heat of a bath the way it does, and takes between fourteen and thirty-two weeks to reach you.

What does it weigh — and will my floor carry it?

Empty weights run from roughly 300 kg to 1,315 kg. The spread is not arbitrary: the translucent onyx vessels are carved deliberately thin — around 30–35 mm at the wall — because that is what lets light pass through the stone, while Erebus is left massive in the river stone it came from. Filled, with a bather in it, add roughly 300 kg.

Because every piece is cut from a natural block, these are indicative. The exact weight of your vessel is measured and confirmed to you in writing, with its footprint and load-spread figures, before any balance is due.

On a ground floor or a concrete slab this is rarely a question. On a suspended timber floor, or anywhere above the ground storey, ask your architect or a structural engineer to confirm the loading before you reserve. We will send the exact weight, footprint and load-spread figures for your piece at no charge — we would far rather you checked than guessed.

How does it connect to plumbing — and do you install it?

The waste outlet is cored and a pop-up waste fitted before dispatch. There is no tap on the vessel itself: the filler is floor- or wall-mounted and chosen by you, which is why rough-in drawings and the waste position are issued at reservation, well before a bathroom is closed up. The connection is made by your own licensed plumber working to those drawings.

The crate comes to your ground-floor threshold. Keep every part of the packaging until the piece is confirmed sound — a transit claim cannot be defended on a crate that went back on the truck. Moving several hundred kilos of stone through a finished house is rigging work rather than delivery — tell us where the piece should stand and we will quote it with a specialist in your city, or coordinate with your builder while the room is still open.

How long does it really take?

Fourteen to twenty-two weeks from reservation for most of the collection. Twenty-four to thirty-two for Aeon, whose petrified wood genuinely cannot be rushed. Roughly half of that is carving and finishing; the rest is ocean freight and clearance.

Air freight removes about four weeks from the shipping leg and is quoted on request. We confirm your dates in writing once your block is selected, and we would rather tell you a true date late than a comfortable one early.

What happens if it chips or cracks?

In transit, nothing is at your risk. The shipment is insured in transit. If a piece arrives damaged, refuse the delivery — do not sign for it. That refusal is what lets us claim against the insurer and the carrier; accepting a damaged piece closes the claim. From there we manage it entirely, and you deal only with us.

Once the piece is in the house, our cover ends — the insurance is on the journey, not on the stone's life afterwards, and there is no separate structural guarantee. Small edge chips in natural stone are repairable in place; we will advise, and where we can we will point you to a stone restorer in your region. Natural fissures, colour movement and vein lines are characteristics of the material rather than faults; they are the reason you chose stone.

Can I choose the stone — or commission something else entirely?

You can. Each of the fourteen is shown in the stone the form was drawn for, but most can be cut from an alternative block. Say so at reservation and we will send photographs of what is available and re-quote if the material changes the price.
